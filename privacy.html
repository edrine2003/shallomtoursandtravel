At Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

By using our website or making a booking with us, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

1 Who We Are

Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels is a licensed tour and travel company based in Kampala, Uganda. We are the data controller responsible for the personal information collected through this website and in connection with our services.

2 What Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Give Us

When you make an enquiry, submit a booking form, contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp, or interact with this website, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Nationality and passport details (for booking with national parks and airlines)
  • Travel dates and destination preferences
  • Number of travellers and ages
  • Dietary requirements and medical conditions relevant to your tour
  • Payment information (processed securely — we do not store card details)
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide in messages or forms

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Your IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search terms used to find us
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This information is collected using cookies and similar technologies. See Section 7 for more details.

2.3 Information From Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties such as travel agents, group organisers or referring partners who make bookings on your behalf. We only use this information to fulfil your booking.

Sensitive Documents: Never send passport numbers, national ID numbers, financial account details or other sensitive personal documents through public web forms, social media, or unsecured chat platforms. Contact us directly for guidance on secure submission.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

Processing Bookings

To confirm, manage and fulfil your tour booking and travel arrangements.

Communication

To respond to your enquiries, send booking confirmations and travel updates.

Payments

To process payments and handle refunds or financial adjustments.

Legal & Safety

To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the safety of our clients and staff.

Service Improvement

To improve our website, services and customer experience based on feedback and usage data.

Marketing

To send promotional content and offers — only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time.

We will not use your personal information for any purpose not listed above without notifying you first and, where required by law, obtaining your consent.

4 Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only where necessary with:

Recipient Purpose
Hotels & Lodges To confirm accommodation reservations on your behalf
Airlines & Transport To book flights, transfers and ground transport
Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) To purchase gorilla / chimpanzee trekking permits
National Park Authorities For park entry registration and permits
Local Guides & Ground Handlers To coordinate on-the-ground logistics for your tour
Payment Processors To securely process your payments
Legal / Regulatory Authorities Where required by law or to protect legal rights

All third parties who receive your data are required to handle it responsibly and only for the purposes stated.

4.1 International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Uganda (for example, when booking with international airlines or hotels). We take steps to ensure adequate protection is in place for such transfers.

5 Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, disclosure or destruction. These include:

  • Secure communication channels for transmitting sensitive information
  • Restricted access to personal data — only staff who need it to do their job can access it
  • Regular review of our data handling practices

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5.1 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting and reporting requirements. Booking records are typically retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance. Marketing data is retained until you withdraw consent.

6 Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Correction

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Deletion

Request that we delete your data where there is no compelling reason to keep it.

Objection

Object to us processing your data for direct marketing purposes.

Restriction

Ask us to restrict how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Portability

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@shallomugandatours.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

6.1 Withdrawing Consent

Where we process your data based on your consent (e.g. for marketing), you may withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

7 Cookies

Our website may use cookies — small text files stored on your device — to improve your browsing experience. We use:

Cookie Type Purpose
Essential Required for the website to function (e.g. form submissions, navigation). Cannot be disabled.
Analytics Help us understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, time on site). Used to improve content.
Preference Remember your settings and preferences for a better experience on return visits.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the website. For more information on managing cookies, visit aboutcookies.org.

7.1 Google Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts to display text. When you visit the site, your browser may send a request to Google's servers to load the font files. Google may collect your IP address in this process. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

8 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us immediately at info@shallomugandatours.com and we will delete that information promptly.

When children are part of a tour booking, their details (name, age, passport) are collected as part of the group booking made by the responsible adult. These details are used solely to fulfil the booking.

9 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as hotels, airlines, Uganda Wildlife Authority or social media platforms). These websites have their own privacy policies, and we have no control over or responsibility for their content or practices.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit from a link on our site.

WhatsApp & Social Media: When you contact us via WhatsApp, Facebook or other platforms, your interactions are also governed by the privacy policies of those platforms. Please review them before sharing sensitive personal information through those channels.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our website or services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
