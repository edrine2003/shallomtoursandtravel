Who We Are

Uganda's Pearl,
Your Adventure

Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels is a licensed, Kampala-based tour and travel company founded with a passion for sharing the extraordinary beauty and biodiversity of Uganda — the Pearl of Africa — with the world.

We specialise in crafting personalised safari experiences, gorilla trekking expeditions, wildlife tours, cultural visits and beach holidays across Uganda, East Africa and beyond. From the misty forests of Bwindi to the thundering waters of Murchison Falls, we bring Uganda's most breathtaking destinations to life.

What sets us apart is not just where we take you — it is how we take you there. Every itinerary is built around your unique interests, travel dates and budget. Our experienced team of guides, travel coordinators and local experts work together to ensure every detail of your journey is handled with care, professionalism and genuine warmth.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do — from how we plan your trip to how we treat every person we meet along the way.

Integrity

We are honest, transparent and straightforward in everything — our pricing, our itineraries and our communication. No hidden fees, no empty promises. What we say is what you get.

Client First

Every decision we make starts with the client. Your comfort, safety and satisfaction are not afterthoughts — they are the reason we exist. We listen carefully and respond personally.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards in every detail — from the lodges we select and the vehicles we use to the guides we train and the itineraries we craft. Good is never good enough.

Reliability

When you book with us, you can count on us. We show up on time, deliver what we promise and are available to support you throughout your journey — before, during and after.

Respect

We respect our clients, our staff, our local communities and the natural environments we operate in. Responsible tourism is not a marketing phrase for us — it is a commitment we honour daily.

Passion

We genuinely love what we do. Our team is made up of proud Ugandans who are passionate about their country and excited to share it with the world. That enthusiasm is contagious — and it shows in every tour we lead.

Community

Tourism should benefit the people and places it touches. We partner with local guides, stay in community-owned lodges where possible and contribute to conservation efforts that protect Uganda's wildlife for future generations.

Safety

Your safety is non-negotiable. We use well-maintained vehicles, work with certified guides and park authorities, follow all national park regulations and conduct thorough risk assessments before every trip.

The Shallom Difference

Why Choose Shallom Uganda Tours?

Here is what makes us different from every other tour company operating in Uganda.

01

Local Experts, Not Middlemen

We are a Uganda-based company with on-the-ground knowledge that no overseas booking platform can match. Our guides are Ugandan, our relationships with national parks and lodges are direct, and our understanding of local conditions is unmatched. When you book with us, you deal directly with the people who will take care of you.

02

Truly Personalised Itineraries

We do not sell fixed packages from a catalogue. Every itinerary we build is designed around you — your travel dates, budget, group size, interests and physical ability. Whether you want a luxury lodge safari, a budget-friendly adventure or a romantic honeymoon, we build it from scratch to fit you perfectly.

03

End-to-End Service

We handle everything from the moment you contact us to the moment you return home. Flights, accommodation, park permits, transfers, visa guidance, travel tips — it is all taken care of. You simply show up and enjoy. No stress, no logistics headaches.

04

Transparent Pricing

We provide clear, itemised quotes so you know exactly what is included and what is not. No surprise charges on arrival, no hidden fees added at checkout. The price we quote is the price you pay — and we will always find the best value within your budget.

05

24/7 Support During Your Trip

When you are in the field, our team is reachable. If your flight is delayed, a permit needs adjustment or you need any support at all, we are a WhatsApp message away. You are never alone on a Shallom tour — our team is with you from check-in to check-out.

06

Responsible & Sustainable Tourism

We are committed to protecting the destinations we operate in. We follow Uganda Wildlife Authority guidelines strictly, support conservation initiatives, employ local guides and use eco-friendly operators where possible. When you travel with us, your money goes back into the communities and ecosystems that make Uganda special.
