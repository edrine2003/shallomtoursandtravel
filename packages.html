🦍 Gorilla Trekking3 Days / 2 Nights📍 Bwindi Forest

Gorilla Trekking Adventure

Spend one precious hour face-to-face with a wild mountain gorilla family in the ancient mist of Bwindi Impenetrable Forest. One of the most profound wildlife encounters on Earth. Only 8 people per gorilla group per day — permits must be booked well in advance.

Gorilla trekking permit included
Professional guide & ranger escort
Private 4×4 safari vehicle
All park entry fees

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Kampala → Bwindi

Depart Kampala early morning. Scenic drive through Fort Portal and tea plantations. Arrive Bwindi late afternoon. Evening briefing at the lodge. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2
Gorilla Trekking Day

Early morning park headquarters briefing, then trek into the forest with rangers. Spend 1 magical hour with the gorilla family. Return to lodge. Optional community walk or Batwa cultural visit. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3
Bwindi → Kampala

Optional morning nature walk. Begin drive back to Kampala via the Equator crossing at Kayabwe (photo stop). Arrive Kampala evening.

Accommodation

Budget, mid-range and luxury lodge options near the trailheads of Buhoma, Ruhija, Nkuringo or Rushaga sectors. We select your lodge based on gorilla permit allocation and your preference.

✓ What's Included

  • Gorilla trekking permit (USD 800)
  • All park entry and activity fees
  • 2 nights accommodation with full board
  • Private 4×4 safari vehicle & driver-guide
  • Professional gorilla trekking guide
  • Ranger escort in the forest
  • Bottled water throughout
  • Equator crossing stop

✗ What's Excluded

  • International flights to/from Uganda
  • Uganda visa and Yellow Fever certificate
  • Travel insurance (strongly recommended)
  • Drinks, laundry, personal expenses
  • Tips for guides, rangers and lodge staff

Pricing

🐘 Wildlife Safari5 Days / 4 Nights📍 Queen Elizabeth NP

Classic Safari Experience

Uganda's flagship safari — tree-climbing lions, Kazinga Channel hippos, chimpanzee tracking, and the dramatic crater lake drive. This 5-day package covers Queen Elizabeth National Park's greatest highlights with an expert guide.

Kazinga Channel boat cruise
Chimpanzee tracking permit
Tree-climbing lion tracking in Ishasha
All meals and park fees included

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Kampala → Queen Elizabeth NP

Depart Kampala, drive west crossing the Equator. Arrive Queen Elizabeth NP afternoon. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2
Game Drive + Boat Cruise

Early morning game drive in Kasenyi Plains — lions, elephants, buffalo, Uganda kob. Afternoon Kazinga Channel boat cruise — world's highest concentration of hippos, crocodiles and water birds. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3
Chimpanzee Tracking + Crater Lakes

Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge (the "Valley of Apes"). Afternoon scenic crater lake drive with panoramic Rift Valley viewpoints. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4
Ishasha — Tree-climbing Lions

Drive to Ishasha sector in search of Uganda's famous tree-climbing lions resting in fig trees. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight.

Day 5
Return to Kampala

Optional morning game drive, then drive back to Kampala. Stop at craft markets. Arrive Kampala evening.

✓ What's Included

  • All park entry fees
  • Kazinga Channel boat cruise
  • Chimpanzee tracking permit
  • 4 nights accommodation with all meals
  • Professional safari guide
  • Private 4×4 safari vehicle
  • Bottled water throughout

✗ What's Excluded

  • International flights
  • Uganda visa and Yellow Fever certificate
  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks, laundry, personal expenses
  • Tips for guides and lodge staff

🌊 Falls Safari4 Days / 3 Nights📍 Murchison Falls NP

Murchison Falls Safari

Witness the world's most powerful waterfall as the Victoria Nile explodes through a 7-metre gorge. Game drives among giraffes, lions and elephants, a boat cruise to the base of the thundering falls, and an optional rhino tracking experience at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary.

Boat cruise to base of falls
Hike to top of Murchison Falls
Game drives — lions, giraffes, elephants
Optional Ziwa rhino tracking

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Kampala → Murchison Falls NP

Depart Kampala. Optional stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary for rhino tracking on foot (USD 50 extra). Continue to the park. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2
Game Drive + Boat Cruise

Early morning game drive on the northern bank — giraffes, elephants, lions, hartebeest, buffalo. Afternoon boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls — hippos, crocodiles and water birds at close range. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3
Top of the Falls + Delta Cruise

Morning hike to the top of Murchison Falls for spectacular views of the gorge. Optional afternoon Nile delta boat cruise to search for the rare shoebill stork. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4
Return to Kampala

Morning game drive, then drive back to Kampala. Stop at Ziwa if not visited on Day 1. Arrive Kampala evening.

✓ What's Included

  • All park entry fees
  • Boat cruise to base of falls
  • Hike to top of falls
  • 3 nights accommodation with all meals
  • Professional safari guide
  • Private 4×4 safari vehicle
  • Bottled water throughout

✗ What's Excluded

  • International flights
  • Uganda visa and Yellow Fever certificate
  • Travel insurance
  • Ziwa rhino tracking (USD 50 optional)
  • Drinks, laundry, personal expenses
  • Tips for guides and lodge staff

🏖️ Beach Holiday7 Days / 6 Nights📍 Zanzibar, Tanzania

Zanzibar Beach Holiday

The perfect extension after your Uganda safari — white-sand beaches, turquoise Indian Ocean, spice farms, Stone Town history, and spectacular sunsets. Combine with any Uganda safari package for the ultimate East Africa experience.

6 nights beach resort
Stone Town & spice farm tours
Dhow sunset cruise included
Jozani Forest & Prison Island

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Arrive Zanzibar

Fly from Entebbe via Dar es Salaam. Transfer to beach resort. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight.

Day 2
Beach Relaxation

Full day at the beach. Optional snorkelling at Mnemba Atoll. Dinner and overnight.

Day 3
Stone Town Tour

Historic tour of Stone Town — ancient Arab doors, House of Wonders, spice market, Forodhani Gardens. Return to beach resort. Dinner and overnight.

Day 4
Spice Farm Tour

Half-day spice farm tour — clove, nutmeg, cinnamon and vanilla. Afternoon beach time. Dinner and overnight.

Day 5
Prison Island + Dhow Cruise

Prison Island (Changuu) visit — Aldabra giant tortoises. Evening dhow sunset cruise. Dinner and overnight.

Day 6
Jozani Forest

Jozani Forest — rare red colobus monkeys. Afternoon beach time and farewell dinner. Overnight.

Day 7
Departure

Morning at leisure, then transfer to Zanzibar Airport for departure flight.

✓ What's Included

  • 6 nights beach resort (B&D)
  • All transfers within Zanzibar
  • Stone Town guided tour
  • Spice farm tour
  • Prison Island visit
  • Jozani Forest visit
  • Dhow sunset cruise

✗ What's Excluded

  • International and domestic flights
  • Tanzania visa fees
  • Travel insurance
  • Lunches, drinks, personal expenses
  • Optional diving/snorkelling
  • Tips for guides and hotel staff
