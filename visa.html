Visa Types We Assist With

We provide guidance for a range of visa categories

Most Popular

Tourist Visa

Ideal for leisure travel, safaris, gorilla trekking and holiday visits. Valid for a short stay, typically 30–90 days depending on the destination country.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Completed visa application form
  • Return or onward flight ticket
  • Confirmed hotel booking or host letter
  • Proof of sufficient funds (bank statement)
  • Travel insurance certificate
  • Yellow Fever vaccination certificate (for Uganda entry)
Uganda offers a visa on arrival and an e-Visa for many nationalities. We can guide you through the online application at visas.immigrationuganda.go.ug.

Business Visa

For professionals attending meetings, conferences, trade events or exploring business opportunities. Does not permit paid employment.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Completed visa application form
  • Business invitation letter from the host company
  • Letter from your employer confirming your position and purpose of travel
  • Company registration documents (if self-employed)
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Return flight ticket and hotel booking
  • Travel insurance certificate
Business visa requirements vary significantly by country. Contact us early so we have enough time to prepare your documents correctly.

Student Visa

For individuals admitted to a recognised educational institution abroad. Allows an extended stay for the duration of the study programme.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond course end date)
  • Admission / acceptance letter from the institution
  • Proof of tuition fee payment or scholarship letter
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Proof of accommodation at the study destination
  • Proof of sufficient funds for living expenses
  • Medical examination certificate (some countries)
  • Language proficiency test results (where required)
  • Sponsor letter or parental consent (for minors)
Apply well in advance — student visa processing can take 4–12 weeks depending on the destination country.

Work / Employment Visa

For individuals with a confirmed job offer abroad. Usually requires employer sponsorship and is tied to a specific employer or sector.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
  • Signed employment contract or job offer letter
  • Employer's sponsorship or work permit approval
  • Professional qualifications and certificates
  • Updated CV / résumé
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Medical examination certificate
  • Proof of accommodation at destination
Work visa requirements are complex and highly destination-specific. Early preparation and legal advice from an immigration lawyer is recommended.

Transit Visa

Required for passing through certain countries en route to your final destination, even if you don't plan to leave the airport.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passport
  • Confirmed onward flight tickets
  • Visa for final destination country (if applicable)
  • Proof of sufficient funds for transit period
Whether you need a transit visa depends on your nationality and your layover country. We can check this for you.

Family / Dependent Visa

For spouses, children or dependants joining a family member who is a resident, student or worker in another country.

Typical documents required:

  • Valid passports for all applicants
  • Proof of relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificates)
  • Sponsor's visa, residence permit or status documents
  • Sponsor's proof of income and accommodation
  • Sponsor's invitation / sponsorship letter
  • Medical examination certificates (some countries)
Document requirements differ greatly by country. Contact us with your specific situation and we will advise accordingly.

Travel Documentation Guidance

Make sure all your documents are in order before you travel

Passport

  • Must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date
  • Must have at least 2–4 blank pages for stamps
  • Not damaged, torn or water-damaged
  • Renew early — allow 4–6 weeks for passport renewal

Yellow Fever Certificate

  • Mandatory for entry into Uganda and many African countries
  • Get vaccinated at least 10 days before departure
  • Valid for life (since 2016 — no longer 10 years)
  • Carry the original yellow card at all times when travelling

Travel Insurance

  • Strongly recommended for all international travel
  • Should cover medical, evacuation, cancellation and theft
  • Some countries and visa types require proof of insurance
  • Keep a digital and printed copy of your policy

Flight Tickets

  • Most embassies require a confirmed return or onward ticket
  • Some accept a flight reservation (not a paid ticket) for visa purposes
  • Ensure names match your passport exactly
  • We can assist with flight bookings and reservations

Accommodation Proof

  • Hotel booking confirmation or host invitation letter
  • Must show full dates matching your travel itinerary
  • Some embassies check that the booking is genuine
  • We can help with hotel booking confirmations

Bank Statements

  • Usually covering the last 3–6 months
  • Must show sufficient funds for your trip
  • Bank letter may also be required
  • Statements must be stamped and signed by your bank
Pro Tip: Always make certified copies of all your documents and store them separately from the originals. Also save digital copies to a secure cloud storage or email them to yourself.
