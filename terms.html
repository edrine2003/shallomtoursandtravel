1 Bookings & Confirmations

1.1 How to Book

Bookings may be made via WhatsApp, email, phone or through enquiry forms on our website. A booking is not confirmed until you receive a written confirmation from Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels and the required deposit has been received.

1.2 Booking Confirmation

Upon receipt of your deposit, we will issue a written booking confirmation detailing your itinerary, travel dates, inclusions, pricing and any special arrangements agreed. It is your responsibility to check all details carefully and notify us of any errors within 48 hours of receipt.

1.3 Group Bookings

For group bookings of 5 or more persons, one lead traveller must be designated as the group contact. That person accepts these Terms and Conditions on behalf of all group members and is responsible for communicating all relevant information to the group.

1.4 Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for providing accurate personal details (names as they appear on passports, travel dates, dietary requirements, medical conditions) at the time of booking. We are not liable for losses arising from incorrect information provided by you.

2 Payments

2.1 Deposit

A non-refundable deposit of 30% of the total tour cost is required to secure your booking. The exact amount will be stated in your booking confirmation. No booking is guaranteed until this deposit is received.

2.2 Final Payment

The remaining balance is due no later than 30 days before the tour departure date. For bookings made within 30 days of departure, full payment is required at the time of booking.

2.3 Late Payment

If full payment is not received by the due date, we reserve the right to treat your booking as cancelled. In such cases, the cancellation charges set out in Section 3 will apply.

2.4 Payment Methods

We accept payment by bank transfer, mobile money (MTN Mobile Money / Airtel Money) and other methods as agreed in writing. All bank charges, transfer fees or currency conversion costs are the responsibility of the client.

2.5 Pricing

All prices are quoted in United States Dollars (USD) or Ugandan Shillings (UGX) as stated. Prices are subject to change without notice until a deposit is paid and a booking is confirmed in writing. We reserve the right to pass on unavoidable cost increases (fuel surcharges, government taxes, park fees) after booking with reasonable notice.

3 Cancellations & Refunds

3.1 Cancellation by the Client

All cancellations must be made in writing (email or WhatsApp message) to Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels. The following charges apply based on the number of days before the scheduled departure:

Days Before Departure Cancellation Charge
More than 60 daysDeposit only (30%)
31 – 60 days50% of total tour cost
15 – 30 days75% of total tour cost
8 – 14 days90% of total tour cost
7 days or fewer / No show100% of total tour cost

3.2 Gorilla Trekking Permits

Gorilla trekking permits are non-refundable and non-transferable once purchased from Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA). If you cancel a gorilla trekking package, the permit cost cannot be refunded regardless of the notice period given.

3.3 Cancellation by Shallom Uganda Tours

We reserve the right to cancel any tour due to insufficient bookings, force majeure events, safety concerns or circumstances beyond our control. In such cases, we will offer you:

  • An alternative tour of equivalent value, or
  • A full refund of payments made to us (excluding third-party costs that are non-refundable).

We are not responsible for any additional costs you may incur (flights, accommodation, visas) as a result of a cancellation by us.

3.4 Force Majeure

We are not liable for cancellations or changes caused by events beyond our reasonable control, including but not limited to: natural disasters, pandemics, government travel restrictions, civil unrest, terrorism, strikes or extreme weather conditions.

3.5 Refund Processing

Where a refund is due, it will be processed within 14 working days of the cancellation being confirmed in writing. Refunds will be made using the same payment method used to make the original payment.

4 Changes to Bookings

4.1 Changes by the Client

Requests to change travel dates, itinerary or passenger details must be made in writing as early as possible. We will make every effort to accommodate changes but cannot guarantee availability. Changes made within 30 days of departure may be treated as cancellations and new bookings.

4.2 Changes by Shallom Uganda Tours

We reserve the right to make minor changes to itineraries (such as accommodation of equivalent standard, route adjustments) where necessary due to operational requirements or safety. We will notify you of any significant changes as soon as possible.

4.3 Transfer of Booking

You may transfer your booking to another person who meets all booking requirements, subject to written notification at least 14 days before departure and payment of any applicable transfer fees charged by third-party suppliers.

  • You must behave in a manner that does not endanger yourself, other travellers, wildlife or local communities.
  • You are responsible for the supervision of minors in your party at all times.
  • Any damage caused by you or your party to accommodation, vehicles or equipment will be your financial responsibility.
  • You must inform us of any medical conditions, disabilities or dietary requirements before booking.

7 Health, Safety & Insurance

7.1 Travel Insurance

We strongly recommend that all clients purchase comprehensive travel insurance before departure. Your policy should cover, at minimum:

  • Trip cancellation and curtailment
  • Medical expenses and emergency evacuation
  • Personal accident and liability
  • Loss or theft of personal belongings

Shallom Uganda Tours and Travels does not provide or sell travel insurance. You travel without insurance at your own risk.

7.2 Vaccinations & Health Requirements

You are responsible for ensuring you meet all health requirements for your destination including vaccinations (a valid Yellow Fever certificate is required for entry into Uganda). Consult your doctor or a travel health clinic well before departure.

7.3 Gorilla Trekking Health Rules

Uganda Wildlife Authority (UWA) requires that anyone with cold, flu or any infectious illness may not trek with gorillas. No refund will be given for permits if you are unable to trek due to health reasons on the day.

7.4 Physical Fitness

Some activities (gorilla trekking, mountain hiking) require a reasonable level of physical fitness. By booking, you confirm that all participants are fit enough to undertake the activities chosen.

Important: Do not send passport numbers, national ID numbers or other sensitive personal documents through public chat forms or social media. Contact us directly for secure document submission guidance.
