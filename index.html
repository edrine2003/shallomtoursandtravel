Murchison Falls National Park

📍 Northwestern Uganda, on the Victoria Nile

Uganda's largest and oldest national park, where the mighty Nile River explodes through a narrow 7-metre gorge before plunging 43 metres into the Devils Cauldron below. The park protects 76 mammal species and over 450 bird species across its 3,893 km² of savanna, riverine forest and woodland.

🦁 Activities

  • Game drives (elephants, giraffes, lions, leopards, buffalo)
  • Boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls
  • Hike to the top of the falls
  • Nile delta cruise (shoebill stork, papyrus vegetation)
  • Bird watching (450+ species including rare shoebill)
  • Sport fishing (Nile perch, tiger fish)

🚗 Travel Info

  • Distance from Kampala: ~305 km (4-5 hour drive)
  • Best time: June–September & December–February (dry season)
  • Park entry fee: USD 40 per person per day
  • Boat cruise: USD 30 per person
  • Accommodation: Budget to luxury lodges available

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

📍 Southwestern Uganda, Kanungu District

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to nearly half of the world's remaining mountain gorillas (~459 individuals). This ancient rainforest dating back 25,000 years shelters 120 mammal species, 348 bird species and 220 butterfly species within its misty, impenetrable canopy.

🦍 Activities

  • Gorilla trekking (the park's flagship experience)
  • Gorilla habituation experience (4 hours with a gorilla family)
  • Nature walks and forest hiking
  • Bird watching (23 Albertine Rift endemics)
  • Batwa cultural community visits
  • Waterfall walks (Munyaga, Rushura trails)

🚗 Travel Info

  • Distance from Kampala: ~460 km (8-10 hour drive)
  • Best time: June–August & December–February (drier trails)
  • Gorilla permit: USD 800 per person (must book in advance)
  • Park entry fee: USD 40 per person per day
  • Accommodation: Budget, mid-range and luxury lodges near trailheads
  • Fitness: Moderate to high fitness required for trekking

Queen Elizabeth National Park

📍 Western Uganda, spanning Kasese, Kamwenge and Rubirizi districts

Uganda's most popular tourist destination, spanning 1,978 km² of diverse ecosystems including savanna, humid forests, lakes and wetlands. Famous for tree-climbing lions in Ishasha, the world's largest concentration of hippos in the Kazinga Channel, and the dramatic crater lakes of the Western Rift Valley.

🐘 Activities

  • Game drives (lions, elephants, buffalo, leopards, Uganda kob)
  • Kazinga Channel boat cruise (hippos, crocodiles, water birds)
  • Tree-climbing lion tracking in Ishasha sector
  • Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge
  • Crater lake drive and viewpoints
  • Bird watching (600+ recorded species)

🚗 Travel Info

  • Distance from Kampala: ~420 km (6-7 hour drive)
  • Best time: January–February & June–September
  • Park entry fee: USD 40 per person per day
  • Boat cruise: USD 30 per person
  • Chimp tracking permit: USD 50 per person
  • Accommodation: Wide range from campsites to luxury lodges

Lake Bunyonyi

📍 Southwestern Uganda, Kabale District

Meaning "Place of Many Little Birds," Lake Bunyonyi is Africa's second-deepest lake at 900 metres and one of the safest lakes in Africa (no bilharzia, no hippos, no crocodiles). Its 29 islands dot the serene waters, surrounded by terraced green hills — a perfect place to relax after a gorilla trek in nearby Bwindi.

🛶 Activities

  • Canoeing and boat rides across 29 islands
  • Swimming (safe, bilharzia-free water)
  • Island hopping and cultural village tours
  • Bird watching (over 200 species)
  • Hiking through terraced hillsides
  • Sunset cruises and overnight island camps

🚗 Travel Info

  • Distance from Kampala: ~430 km (7-8 hour drive)
  • Best time: June–September & December–February
  • No park entry fee required
  • Boat rides: USD 10–25 per person
  • Near Bwindi: only 1-2 hour drive to gorilla trailheads
  • Accommodation: Eco-lodges, island resorts, budget camps

Zanzibar — The Spice Island

📍 Indian Ocean, off the coast of Tanzania

An archipelago of white-sand beaches, turquoise waters and rich Swahili culture. Zanzibar combines the historic Stone Town (a UNESCO World Heritage Site) with world-class beach resorts in Nungwi, Kendwa, Pongwe and Matemwe — the ideal beach extension after a Uganda safari.

🏖️ Activities

  • Beach relaxation and swimming
  • Snorkelling and scuba diving (Mnemba Atoll)
  • Stone Town historic tour (SPICE markets, doors, alleys)
  • Spice farm tours
  • Dhow sunset cruises
  • Jozani Forest (red colobus monkey) visit
  • Prison Island giant tortoises

✈️ Travel Info

  • Flights: Entebbe (EBB) to Zanzibar (ZNZ) — connection via Dar es Salaam or direct seasonal flights
  • Best time: June–October (dry, cooler) & December–February
  • Visa: Tanzania e-visa or visa on arrival for most nationalities
  • Yellow fever certificate required if coming from Uganda
  • Accommodation: Beach resorts from budget to 5-star luxury

Gorilla Trekking Adventure

📍 Bwindi Impenetrable NP ⏱️ 3 Days / 2 Nights 👥 Group max 8

An unforgettable journey into the misty mountains of Bwindi to spend a precious hour face-to-face with a family of wild mountain gorillas — one of the most profound wildlife encounters on Earth.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Depart Kampala early (or fly to Kihihi airstrip). Drive through scenic countryside to Bwindi Impenetrable National Park. Evening briefing at the lodge. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 2: Early morning briefing at the park headquarters, then gorilla trekking (2–6 hours depending on gorilla location). Spend 1 magical hour with the gorilla family. Return to lodge, optional community walk. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 3: Optional Batwa cultural experience or nature walk, then drive back to Kampala (or fly from Kihihi). Stop at the Equator for photos. Arrive Kampala evening.

Accommodation

Options available from budget guesthouses to luxury lodges near the trekking trailhead. We select accommodation based on your preference and gorilla permit allocation sector (Buhoma, Ruhija, Nkuringo, or Rushaga).

✓ Included

  • Gorilla trekking permit (USD 800)
  • Park entry fees
  • Professional English-speaking guide
  • 2 nights accommodation with meals
  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle
  • Bottled water in vehicle
  • Community walk (optional)

✗ Excluded

  • International flights to/from Uganda
  • Visa and Yellow Fever certificate
  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks, laundry, personal items
  • Tips for guides and rangers
  • Optional activities not listed

Classic Safari Experience

📍 Queen Elizabeth NP ⏱️ 5 Days / 4 Nights 👥 Group max 12

Uganda's flagship safari — game drives in search of tree-climbing lions, a boat cruise among hippos and crocodiles, chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge, and the spectacular crater lake drive.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Depart Kampala, drive to Queen Elizabeth NP via the Equator crossing. Lunch en route. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight at lodge.
  • Day 2: Early morning game drive in the Kasenyi plains (lions, elephants, buffalo, Uganda kob). Afternoon boat cruise on the Kazinga Channel — world's highest concentration of hippos. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 3: Chimpanzee tracking in Kyambura Gorge. Afternoon crater lake drive with spectacular viewpoints over the Western Rift Valley. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 4: Drive to Ishasha sector for tree-climbing lion tracking. Picnic lunch in the park. Afternoon game drive. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 5: Morning nature walk or optional game drive, then drive back to Kampala. Stop at craft markets. Arrive Kampala evening.

Accommodation

Mid-range lodges and tented camps inside or near the park, with options to upgrade to luxury lodges overlooking the Kazinga Channel or the Rift Valley.

✓ Included

  • All park entry fees
  • Kazinga Channel boat cruise
  • Chimpanzee tracking permit
  • 4 nights accommodation with all meals
  • Professional safari guide
  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle
  • Bottled water and park rangers

✗ Excluded

  • International flights
  • Visa and Yellow Fever certificate
  • Travel insurance
  • Drinks, laundry, personal items
  • Tips for guides and lodge staff

Murchison Falls Safari

📍 Murchison Falls NP ⏱️ 4 Days / 3 Nights 👥 Group max 12

Witness the Nile River explode through a 7-metre gorge at the world's most powerful waterfall. Game drives among giraffes, lions and elephants, plus a boat cruise to the base of the thundering falls.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Depart Kampala, drive north through Luwero and Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary (optional rhino tracking). Continue to Murchison Falls NP. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 2: Early morning game drive on the northern bank (giraffes, elephants, lions, hartebeest, buffalo). Afternoon boat cruise to the base of Murchison Falls — see hippos, crocodiles and water birds. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 3: Hike to the top of Murchison Falls for spectacular views. Optional delta boat cruise to search for the shoebill stork. Afternoon game drive or relaxation at the lodge. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 4: Morning game drive or optional activity, then drive back to Kampala. Stop at Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary if not visited on Day 1. Arrive Kampala evening.

Accommodation

Lodges and camps inside the park and along the Nile riverbank — from budget campsites to luxury lodges with river views.

✓ Included

  • All park entry fees
  • Boat cruise to base of falls
  • Hike to top of falls
  • 3 nights accommodation with meals

  • Private 4x4 safari vehicle
  • Bottled water

✗ Excluded

Zanzibar Beach Holiday

📍 Zanzibar, Tanzania ⏱️ 7 Days / 6 Nights 👥 All group sizes

The perfect beach extension after your Uganda safari — white-sand beaches, turquoise Indian Ocean waters, historic Stone Town, spice farms and unforgettable sunsets.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive Zanzibar (from Entebbe via connecting flight). Transfer to beach resort in Nungwi or Kendwa. Evening at leisure. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 2: Full day beach relaxation. Optional snorkelling at Mnemba Atoll. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 3: Stone Town historic tour — ancient Arab doors, House of Wonders, spice market, Forodhani Gardens. Dinner and overnight at beach resort.
  • Day 4: Spice farm tour — learn about Zanzibar's clove, nutmeg and cinnamon heritage. Afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 5: Prison Island (Changuu) visit — giant Aldabra tortoises. Afternoon dhow sunset cruise. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 6: Jozani Forest — rare red colobus monkeys. Afternoon beach time and farewell dinner. Dinner and overnight.
  • Day 7: Morning at leisure, then transfer to Zanzibar Airport for departure flight.

Accommodation

Beach resorts from budget to 5-star luxury in Nungwi, Kendwa, Pongwe or Matemwe. All with ocean views, swimming pools and full-board options.

✓ Included

  • 6 nights beach resort accommodation
  • Daily breakfast and dinner
  • All transfers in Zanzibar
  • Stone Town tour
  • Spice farm tour
  • Prison Island visit
  • Jozani Forest visit
  • Dhow sunset cruise

✗ Excluded

  • International flights to/from Zanzibar
  • Tanzania visa fees
  • Travel insurance
  • Lunches, drinks, personal items
  • Optional diving/snorkelling
  • Tips for guides and hotel staff
