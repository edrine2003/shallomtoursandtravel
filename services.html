Our Travel Services

More than just tours — we handle every detail of your journey, from flights and hotels to visas and airport transfers, so you can focus on the experience.

Flight Bookings

We assist with both domestic and international flight bookings, finding the best routes and prices for your travel dates. Whether you need a direct flight to Entebbe or a connection through Nairobi or Addis Ababa, our team will handle the search and booking on your behalf.

  • International flights to/from Entebbe (EBB)
  • Domestic flights (Kampala, Kihihi, Kasese, Pakuba, Kisoro)
  • Connections through Nairobi, Addis Ababa, Dubai
  • Flight reservations for visa applications
  • Group booking discounts
  • Flight + tour combo packages
  • Flights to Zanzibar (via Dar es Salaam)
  • Assistance with flight changes and rebooking

Hotel Reservations

We have established relationships with accommodation providers across Uganda, Tanzania and East Africa — from budget guesthouses and eco-lodges to luxury safari camps and 5-star beach resorts. We find the right fit for your budget and location, and handle all bookings on your behalf.

  • Budget guesthouses and backpacker lodges
  • Mid-range 3-star hotels and tented camps
  • Comfort 4-star safari lodges
  • Luxury 5-star premium lodges and resorts
  • Beach resorts in Zanzibar and East Africa coast
  • Lodges near national park trailheads
  • City hotels in Kampala, Entebbe, Kabale
  • Hotel confirmations for visa applications
Enquire About Hotels →

Car Hire & Airport Transfers

We provide reliable private transport for airport pickups, drop-offs, and self-drive or chauffeur-driven car hire across Uganda. All vehicles are well-maintained 4x4s suitable for safari roads and Uganda's terrain.

  • Entebbe Airport pickups and drop-offs
  • Private transfers — Kampala to any destination
  • Safari 4x4 vehicles (Land Cruiser, Safari Van)
  • Self-drive car hire with or without GPS
  • Minibuses for group transfers (up to 25 people)
  • Long-distance cross-border transfers
  • Kampala city transfers and day hire
  • 24/7 roadside support during your hire
Enquire About Transport →

Group & Corporate Travel

We specialise in planning and coordinating travel for groups of all sizes — family reunions, school trips, church groups, corporate incentive travel, conference delegates, and NGO field teams. We manage every detail from group gorilla permits to multi-vehicle convoys.

  • Group gorilla and chimpanzee permits
  • Multi-vehicle group safari coordination
  • Group accommodation at negotiated rates
  • Family and honeymoon packages
  • Corporate incentive travel and team building
  • Conference and event logistics
  • NGO and humanitarian travel support
  • School and university field trips

Holiday Planning

Not sure where to start? Our holiday planning service builds a complete, bespoke travel experience around your interests, travel dates, budget and group size — from a weekend getaway to a month-long East Africa expedition. We handle everything so you just show up and enjoy.

  • Personalised itinerary design
  • Multi-destination East Africa holidays
  • Safari + beach combo holidays
  • Honeymoon and anniversary packages
  • Family holidays with children
  • Adventure and hiking holidays
  • Cultural and community tourism
  • Budget and luxury holiday options
